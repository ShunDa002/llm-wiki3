A question page exists so an unanswered question survives. Without one, a source that raises a
real uncertainty but states no claim produces either a concept page asserting something the
source never said, or nothing at all — and "nothing at all" means the vault quietly forgets what
it does not know.

Do not create one for every passing uncertainty. A question earns a page when answering it would
change a decision, or when more than one source has now bumped into it.

# <Question, as a question>

## The question

State it as a question, in one sentence.

## Why it matters

What decision, project, or claim is waiting on the answer.

## What the vault knows now

- What is established, with the page or source that establishes it: [[Source - <Name>]]

## What the vault does not know

- The specific gap. Name the evidence that would close it, not just the absence.

## Do not answer this from

- [[<Page>]] — and why it does not apply. A synthesis scoped to a small vault is not evidence
  about a large one, and this line is what stops the next query from borrowing it anyway.

## Answer

Empty while `status: open`. When answered, record the answer, the evidence, and set
`answered_by` to the page that carries it.
